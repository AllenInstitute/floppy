**mIF-procedures.json**

**True schema gaps (missing models/fields):**

-   **No cyclic IF series model** --- The schema has HCRSeries for
    cyclic mFISH rounds but nothing equivalent for antibody-based cyclic
    IF (Cell DIVE). Each round is a separate SpecimenProcedure as a
    workaround; a CyclicIFSeries model is needed.

-   **Antibody model is insufficient** --- FluorescentStain/ProteinProbe
    don\'t support: antibody clone, working concentration in µg/mL
    (schema uses mass, not concentration), or institutional barcodes
    (expected/scanned/match). RRID is the only formal identifier. The
    antibody\_channels structure in protocol\_parameters is a
    workaround.

-   **No deactivation step model** --- H₂O₂ bleaching between rounds has
    no structured schema representation. Currently a custom object in
    protocol\_parameters.

-   **No panel assignment field** --- SpecimenProcedure has no field to
    record which antibody panel (Panel A vs. Panel B) was applied to
    which slide within a round. Currently free-text only.

-   **No tissue collection procedure type** --- SpecimenProcedureType
    has no entry for human donor tissue block acquisition. Would fall
    under OTHER, losing semantic specificity.

**Structural conflict:**

-   **procedure\_type field mismatch** --- All procedures use
    procedure\_type: \"SpecimenProcedure\" (the class name) plus a
    non-existent procedure\_type\_name field. Schema requires
    procedure\_type to be a SpecimenProcedureType enum value and uses
    procedure\_name for free-text. Needs correction across all
    procedures.

-   **protocol\_id format mismatch** --- Field expects a protocols.io
    DOI; AIFI uses SLIMS IDs. Either AIFI protocols need protocols.io
    DOIs, or the schema needs to support institutional IDs.

**mIF-acquisition.json**

-   **Round 1.5 has no representation** --- A non-integer staining round
    (second antibody pass between Rounds 1 and 2) can\'t be cleanly
    expressed. Options: fold into Round 1, treat as its own acquisition
    with a non-integer ID, or add a protocol deviation field. Needs
    discussion.

-   **Cell DIVE imaging parameters unsupported** ---
    ImagingConfig/DetectorConfig supports per-channel exposure time, but
    Cell DIVE-specific imaging parameters have no schema representation
    and would require extension.

**mIF-data\_description.json**

-   **Restrictions field underspecified** --- No guidance on whether
    legal contract IDs belong here; restriction categories are
    undefined. Patient consent may overlap with the subject schema.

**mIF-subject.json**

-   **Missing human subject fields** --- No schema support for: multiple
    diagnoses with time-based updates, ethnicity, race, or consent. Also
    unresolved: whether cohort belongs at the subject or sample level.
