**Gap Analysis**

**AICS Primary-Image Metadata Schema vs. AIND Data Schema v2.9.0**

Comparison of the AICS REMBI-categorized slim primary-image metadata
view against the canonical AIND v2.9.0 JSON schema, with architectural
findings, field mappings, readiness gaps, and migration recommendations.

**Status:** Draft for scientific and engineering review

**Prepared:** August 25, 2026

**Reference schema:** aind-data-schema v2.9.0 (final release) -
https://github.com/AllenNeuralDynamics/aind-data-schema/tree/v2.9.0

**Source schema:** AICS candidate schema 0.6.0; 33,184-row, 186-field
slim REMBI-categorized primary-image Parquet view

**Companion evidence:** Complete top-level, nested-child, and
AIND-required-field matrices are provided in the accompanying Excel
workbook.

**1. Purpose and Scope**
========================

This document compares the supplied AICS primary-image metadata schema
with aind-data-schema v2.9.0. The source is a discovery-oriented Parquet
view: one row per retained primary-image file, with 186 top-level fields
and four nested objects containing 49 physical child fields. The
reference is a hierarchical set of JSON models centered on a data asset
produced by one acquisition.

**Scope boundary.** The analysis evaluates schema semantics, structure,
requiredness, type/cardinality, controlled vocabularies, and feasible
transformations. It does not claim that a mapping is populated for every
row. Existing AICS validation evidence establishes the view\'s shape and
internal consistency; AIND readiness is assessed separately.

**Version boundary.** The reference is pinned to v2.9.0, the final
aind-data-schema release. Future evolution occurs in the biodata-schema
repository, so migration decisions should not be based on an unpinned
\'latest\' URL.

**Interpretive principle.** A field is considered direct only when its
scientific meaning and destination are materially aligned. A flexible
notes or parameters dictionary is classified as an extension container,
not a true semantic match.

**2. Executive Summary of Key Gaps**
====================================

The schemas overlap on imaging concepts, but they were designed around
different units of registration and different scientific domains. Eleven
of 186 top-level AICS fields have a direct destination; 75 are partial,
48 require flexible extension containers, 23 have no first-class analog,
10 belong in a file-manifest or platform layer, and 19 are search-only
derivatives that should be regenerated rather than migrated.

  **\#**   **Gap**                                                                                                                                          **Impact**
  -------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **1**    Asset boundary: one AICS row is one image file; one AIND data asset represents an acquisition-level collection.                                  High - identity, location, and every repeated field depend on the aggregation rule.
  **2**    Biological identity: AICS models cell lines, clones, and lineage; AIND v2.9.0 models animal/human Subjects and derived specimen IDs.             High - required subject\_id/specimen\_id cannot be populated without a governed profile.
  **3**    AIND acquisition timing is required and timezone-aware; AICS dates are heterogeneous and often date-only.                                        High - acquisition and DataStream objects may fail validation or misstate events.
  **4**    Instrument/config referential integrity requires named cameras, detectors, lasers, triggers, and coordinate systems.                             High - objective facts alone cannot construct a complete Instrument plus DataStream.
  **5**    Plate, well, layout, colony, and experimental-condition metadata have no first-class AIND model.                                                 High - core experimental design would become untyped parameters or be lost.
  **6**    Generic image file metadata (format, TCZYX shape, axis order, byte size, previews) exceeds AIND\'s acquisition-level image model.                High - a governed file manifest or imaging extension is needed.
  **7**    Culture, preparation, treatments, concentrations, and timepoints exceed the AIND Reagent and SpecimenProcedure vocabularies.                     Medium/High - flexible parameters preserve values but not interoperable semantics.
  **8**    Processing fields lack the code URLs, experimenters, timestamps, ordering, and dependency graph required for reproducible DataProcess records.   High - derived-data provenance is only partly constructible.
  **9**    AICS QC flags can become QCMetric records, but modality, stage, metric value, grouping, and status history require normalization.                Medium - quality signals are present but not directly valid AIND QC.
  **10**   Required administrative metadata - institution, funding, investigators, creation semantics - is not present or is ambiguous.                     High - DataDescription cannot be validated from the supplied view alone.

**Mapping evidence summary**
----------------------------

  **Classification**          **Top-level fields**   **Interpretation**
  --------------------------- ---------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Direct**                  11                     Meaning aligns; normalization or unit encoding may still be needed.
  **Partial**                 75                     A destination exists, but granularity, meaning, or required context differs.
  **Extension container**     48                     Value can be retained in parameters/notes without first-class semantics.
  **No first-class analog**   23                     AIND v2.9.0 lacks the scientific construct.
  **Platform or manifest**    10                     Belongs with file inventory, storage, preview, or lifecycle services.
  **Derived/search-only**     19                     Regenerate from canonical nested values; do not migrate independently.

**3. Terminology Reconciliation**
=================================

The two schemas reuse common terms at different levels. The following
vocabulary is used throughout this analysis.

  **AICS term**                      **Closest AIND concept**                                 **Caveat**
  ---------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Asset / Parquet row**            Image file within an AIND data asset                     AICS row identity is finer-grained than AIND asset identity.
  **Acquisition group**              Acquisition + DataDescription.name                       Best aggregation key, but AIND identity is centered on acquisition time and subject/specimen.
  **Dataset / collection / study**   DataDescription.project\_name, name, tags                No identified Study or Collection object in v2.9.0.
  **Cell line**                      Subject/specimen profile (no direct object)              A cell line is not an animal/human Subject and cannot safely be substituted for one.
  **Plate / well / layout**          SpecimenProcedure.protocol\_parameters                   Possible storage location only; not a typed experimental-design model.
  **Channel description**            ImagingConfig.channels\[\].Channel                       AIND also requires a detector configuration and named devices.
  **Reagent / treatment**            Reagent + SpecimenProcedure or Manipulation              AIND Reagent lacks role, concentration, volume, group, and treatment timing.
  **Output**                         Processing.DataProcess output\_path/output\_parameters   Viewer and download URLs remain platform/file-manifest metadata.
  **Search bridge**                  Search index derived from canonical metadata             Not portable scientific metadata; regenerate after migration.

**4. High-Level and Architectural Differences**
===============================================

**4.1 Unit of registration and asset boundary**
-----------------------------------------------

The largest structural difference is not a field-name mismatch. The AICS
view registers each primary image file independently, while AIND
describes one acquisition episode and its data asset as a collection.
Mapping each file row to a separate AIND asset would duplicate
acquisition, instrument, specimen, treatment, and processing metadata
and could invent artificial sessions. Aggregating rows requires a
deterministic rule and a companion file manifest.

  **Dimension**      **AICS source**                               **AIND v2.9.0**                               **Consequence**
  ------------------ --------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------
  **Unit**           One primary-image file row                    One acquisition-level data asset              Define aggregation/decomposition.
  **Identity**       metadata\_record\_id, source IDs, file path   metadata.name + acquisition time/subject      Preserve row IDs as secondary identifiers.
  **Location**       Per-file path                                 Single asset root location                    Derive root; retain relative file paths.
  **Multiplicity**   Many rows may share acquisition\_group\_id    One Acquisition with streams/configs/images   Group before validation.
  **Derived data**   Nested outputs plus file roles                Processing/DataProcess and source\_data       Build explicit lineage graph.

**4.2 Biological identity and specimen semantics**
--------------------------------------------------

AICS is cell-line-centric: cell line, parental line, clone, tagged
structure, genes/proteins, reagents, and culture state are central. AIND
v2.9.0 requires acquisition.subject\_id and, for in-vitro modalities,
specimen\_id, but its Subject detail types are animal/human-oriented and
its specimen model is primarily expressed through procedure identifiers.
Treating a cell-line identifier as subject\_id would be syntactically
convenient but semantically misleading.

**Required decision.** Define a versioned AICS cell-line/specimen
profile with stable IDs, relationships to organism/donor where known,
clone lineage, derivation provenance, and an explicit policy for
populating AIND subject\_id and specimen\_id.

**4.3 Entity and hierarchy comparison**
---------------------------------------

  **Concern**               **AICS**                                        **AIND v2.9.0**                                  **Assessment**
  ------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------------------------------
  **Dataset grouping**      dataset\_name, dataset\_id, collection, study   DataDescription project/name/tags                Partial; identifiers and study semantics are weaker.
  **Experimental layout**   plate, well, colony, condition                  No first-class model                             Schema/profile gap.
  **Instrument**            System + objective scalars                      Instrument components + named devices            Partial; device inventory is incomplete.
  **Acquisition**           Repeated per-file settings                      Acquisition -\> DataStreams -\> configurations   Requires grouping and normalization.
  **Image header**          Axis, shape, sizes, voxel dimensions            Selected Image classes and transforms            Partial; no generic TCZYX manifest.
  **Processing**            Flat fields + nested outputs                    Ordered DataProcess records + Code               Partial; reproducibility fields missing.
  **Quality**               Status, flags, notes                            QCMetric objects + grouping/status               Constructible with normalization.

**4.4 Extensibility and governance**
------------------------------------

AIND\'s typed objects and registries give shared fields strong
semantics, while several escape hatches - protocol\_parameters,
output\_parameters, code.parameters, additional\_settings, and notes -
can retain project-specific values. Those containers are useful for
migration, but relying on them for 48 top-level AICS fields would
produce technically valid yet weakly interoperable metadata. Stable,
widely used AICS constructs should be promoted into a governed profile
or proposed as schema additions rather than left indefinitely as
anonymous key/value data.

**6. AIND Required-Field Readiness**
====================================

Forty-two high-value AIND required or conditionally required checks were
evaluated for the intended in-vitro imaging pathway. Twenty-eight are
high-severity gaps, nine are medium-severity gaps, and five are
low-severity or directly available. This is not a population percentage;
it is a readiness assessment of the information needed to construct
valid, scientifically meaningful AIND objects.

  **Core object**              **Current readiness**   **Principal blocker**
  ---------------------------- ----------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Metadata**                 Partial                 Asset name and root location require acquisition-level aggregation.
  **DataDescription**          Low                     Institution, funding, investigator identity, creation semantics, and modality mapping.
  **Subject/specimen**         Low                     No compatible cell-line Subject profile or stable specimen-ID policy.
  **Procedures**               Partial                 Culture/treatment details fit only flexible parameters; boundaries and actors are incomplete.
  **Instrument**               Partial                 Objective facts exist; device inventory, modification date, and coordinate system are incomplete.
  **Acquisition/DataStream**   Partial                 Timing, timezone, active-device names, detector trigger, and stream construction.
  **Processing**               Partial                 Code URL/version, experimenters, timestamps, stage, and dependency graph.
  **QualityControl**           Partial                 Metric normalization, modality/stage, values, grouping, and status history.

**Selected validation blockers**
--------------------------------

  **AIND field**                              **Closest AICS evidence**            **Coverage**               **Gap**
  ------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------ -------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **data\_description.institution**           None                                 Missing                    No collecting institution field in the supplied view.
  **data\_description.funding\_source**       None                                 Missing                    No funder/grant metadata.
  **acquisition.subject\_id**                 cell\_line / parental\_cell\_line    Semantic mismatch          A cell line is not an AIND live Subject instance.
  **acquisition.specimen\_id**                None; could be derived               Missing                    Stable specimen identity and derivation rules must be defined.
  **acquisition.acquisition\_end\_time**      duration\_minutes                    Derivable only sometimes   Requires reliable start time plus duration; otherwise absent.
  **DataStream.active\_devices**              instrument\_name; objective\_model   Partial                    AIND requires names matching Instrument components; camera/light-source names are missing.

  **DataProcess.code.url**                    content\_generation\_algorithm       Missing                    Algorithm name is insufficient for reproducibility.
  **quality\_control.default\_grouping**      None                                 Missing                    Grouping policy must be defined for generated metrics.

**7. Cross-Cutting Concerns**
=============================

**7.1 Time, units, and typed values**
-------------------------------------

AIND expects timezone-aware datetimes and explicit units in many
device/configuration models. The AICS view contains a mixture of
strings, dates, numeric values with units encoded in field names, and
raw source text. Migration should parse values into number-plus-unit
pairs, retain raw text for provenance, and reject fabricated timezone or
end-time values.

**7.2 Controlled vocabularies and persistent identifiers**
----------------------------------------------------------

AIND uses registries for modalities, species, organizations, people,
reagents, and units. AICS vocabularies are project-oriented and include
cell-line/catalog provenance. A governed crosswalk is required; values
that do not map should remain explicit extensions rather than being
forced into an approximately named registry entry.

**7.3 Search and presentation fields**
--------------------------------------

Nineteen top-level AICS fields are flattened search bridges derived from
nested cell-line, channel, reagent, or output metadata. They should not
be copied into portable AIND JSON. The registry should index the
canonical nested values and regenerate equivalent search fields.
Likewise, REMBI category prefixes are presentation labels, not
scientific metadata keys.

**7.4 Source-view limitations**
-------------------------------

The supplied Parquet is a slim primary-image view, not the entire
282-field governed AICS class model. It omits 12 fields that are empty
across the selected primary-image rows and excludes non-primary assets
by design. Conclusions about AIND coverage therefore apply to onboarding
this view; they should not be generalized to every AICS asset class
without repeating the comparison against the canonical 198-field and
full class schemas.

**7.5 Data-population evidence**
--------------------------------

The AICS companion validation reports 33,184 rows, 186 top-level fields,
five row groups, full category annotation, preserved values/types, and
no all-empty retained fields. The broader quality baseline reports
98.75% required-field completion under AICS rules. Neither result
guarantees AIND validity because AIND introduces different required
fields, hierarchies, and controlled vocabularies.

**8. Proposed Adjustments and Migration Recommendations**
=========================================================

Recommendations are ordered by dependency. They distinguish changes to
onboarding logic, a governed AICS-to-AIND profile, the registry
platform/file-manifest layer, and potential future schema proposals.

**R1 - Define the AIND asset boundary and aggregation contract**
----------------------------------------------------------------

Group AICS file rows into acquisition-level assets using a governed key
led by acquisition\_group\_id, acquisition timing, instrument, and
biological/plate context. Preserve metadata\_record\_id, file\_name,
relative path, size, and source IDs in a companion file manifest. Do not
create one AIND acquisition per file by default.

**R2 - Create a cell-line/specimen identity profile**
-----------------------------------------------------

Define stable specimen IDs and a cell-line identity object or governed
extension carrying organism, cell line, parental line, clone, tagged
structure, genes/proteins, catalog reference, and derivation provenance.
Document precisely how AIND subject\_id and specimen\_id are populated
without implying a live animal/human Subject.

**R3 - Model plate, well, and experimental design explicitly**
--------------------------------------------------------------

Add a typed registry/profile layer for plate barcode, format, model,
layout, well, well group, colony/position, condition, and batch.
Reference it from the acquisition/specimen package. Avoid burying core
design variables indefinitely in untyped protocol\_parameters.

**R4 - Build an AICS microscopy Instrument/Acquisition profile**
----------------------------------------------------------------

Specify the minimum Microscope, Objective, Camera/Detector, Laser/LED,
DataStream, ImagingConfig, Channel, Image, and coordinate-system
representation. Require stable device names, detector trigger,
acquisition start/end with timezone, modality mappings, and unit
normalization.

**R5 - Add a governed file and image manifest**
-----------------------------------------------

Retain physical format, byte size, source file IDs, previews, TCZYX
shape, axis order, pixel/voxel spacing, scene/FOV/position, and per-file
role beneath the acquisition asset. Keep storage/cache lifecycle fields
in the Registry platform, not portable scientific JSON.

**R6 - Normalize culture, specimen preparation, and treatment events**
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Transform seeding, replate, differentiation, fixation, culture media,
passage, reagents, concentrations, volumes, treatment groups, and timing
into ordered SpecimenProcedure or Manipulation events. Add typed profile
fields for concepts not represented by AIND Reagent/SpecimenProcedure.

**R7 - Construct reproducible Processing and lineage records**
--------------------------------------------------------------

Expand flat analysis/segmentation/output fields into ordered DataProcess
records with process type, stage, Code URL/version/commit, parameters,
experimenters, timestamps, inputs, outputs, and dependency graph.
Preserve source wording when normalized mapping is uncertain.

**R8 - Normalize QC into QCMetric objects**
-------------------------------------------

Define metric names, modality, evaluation stage, typed values, status
history, grouping tags, and references for AICS QC flags. Keep registry
workflow state separate from scientific QC status.

**R9 - Separate portable metadata from index/platform concerns**
----------------------------------------------------------------

Regenerate search bridges from canonical nested values. Keep thumbnail,
cache/cold-storage state, upload audit, local lifecycle, and UI category
labels in the registry/file-manifest layer. Maintain an explicit
crosswalk so no source field silently disappears.

**R10 - Supply missing administrative and version-governance metadata**
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Add institution, funding, investigators with persistent identifiers,
creation-time semantics, project mapping, license/restrictions, and
modality mapping from authoritative dataset-level sources. Pin v2.9.0
for this migration and establish a separate decision for transition to
biodata-schema.

**9. Open Questions**
=====================

-   What is the authoritative AIND data-asset boundary:
    acquisition\_group\_id, plate/well/session, or each physical image
    file?

-   What stable identifier represents a cell-line-derived specimen, and
    how is it related to organism, donor, parental line, and clone?

-   Should plate/well/experimental design live in an AICS profile, the
    Registry relational layer, or a proposed future schema extension?

-   Which legacy acquisitions may use ExternalDataStream because device
    details are unavailable, and what minimum evidence is still
    required?

-   Are generic AIND parameters acceptable as a transitional store, or
    must key AICS fields be typed before onboarding?

-   Which timestamp source is authoritative for acquisition start/end,
    processing, and specimen events, and what timezone policy applies?

-   Should the production target remain aind-data-schema v2.9.0 or move
    directly to the successor biodata-schema after this baseline
    comparison?

-   Who owns and approves controlled-vocabulary mappings, extension
    keys, validation exceptions, and future migration upgrades?

companion files, the supplied Immunology gap analysis as the reporting
template, and the official tagged AIND v2.9.0 source schemas. The

required fields plus the required nested objects used for in-vitro
imaging.

**Method note.** \'Direct\' does not mean the entire AIND object is
valid; it means the individual field has a defensible destination.
\'Extension container\' indicates retention in a flexible dictionary or
notes field and is intentionally not counted as semantic conformance.

would be emitted in an AICS in-vitro imaging package.
